Zikr Timer is a simple, distraction-free interval timer for zikr (dhikr). Choose how long each level lasts and how many levels you want. The app counts you through every level and plays a gentle beep when each one ends, so you can keep your eyes closed and your focus on remembrance.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set the interval in minutes and seconds.
• Set the number of levels.
• The total duration is calculated for you.
• Press Start. A beep marks the end of each level.
• Pause, resume or reset at any time.

FEATURES
• Level countdown: see the elapsed time, the current level and the total duration at a glance.
• Gentle audio cue at the end of every level.
• Session history: every completed or stopped session is saved with its date, interval, levels reached and total time.
• English and Urdu: full Urdu interface with right-to-left layout.
• Light and dark themes: an easy-on-the-eyes dark mode for night-time zikr.
• Remembers your settings: your interval, levels, language and theme are kept for next time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in.
• No ads and no tracking.
• Works fully offline. Your settings and history never leave your device.

Whether you follow a daily wird, a set routine from your sheikh, or simply want a calm way to time your remembrance, Zikr Timer keeps the count so you don't have to.

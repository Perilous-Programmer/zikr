Zikr Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Zikr Timer ("the app") is an offline interval timer for zikr. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Your timer settings: interval length and number of levels.
  • Your preferences: language (English or Urdu) and theme (light or dark).
  • Your session history: the date and time of each session, the interval, the target and completed levels, the total time, and whether the session was completed or stopped.

This information is never sent to us or to any third party. You can delete your session history at any time with "Clear history" in the app, or delete all app data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account or sign-in, contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and does not request the internet permission or any other Android permission.

Device backup

If you have enabled Android's own backup service, Android may include the app's settings and history in your personal device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings and is not accessible to us.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
